How each activity's costs count

  • Earns revenue — the departments in the daily log (and drinks), plus Tiki Taka and the Salon with the sales entered on their pages. Their expenses are direct costs.
  • Shared by all activities — common costs (general, cleaning, technical…), spread over the activities that earn revenue.
  • Its own line — shown separately with its costs, e.g. something that has no revenue in the daily log.
  • Part of … — its costs count for an activity that earns revenue (e.g. tournaments as part of Terrain).
